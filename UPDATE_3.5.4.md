# BrightSteps 3.5.4

- Enlarged Piggy Bank and Treasure Chest so their visual footprint is comparable to the Glass Jar.
- Expanded both internal currency windows and raised their z-index so earned currency remains visibly in front of the translucent container art.
- Restricted the pig/chest front overlays to the lower perimeter so they create an "inside the container" effect without hiding earned currency.
- Re-spaced coin/marble/star/point positions into wider bottom-up rows to prevent clipping and excessive overlap.
- Increased coin/marble bitmap size slightly while retaining fixed-size images during animation.
- Smoothed and lengthened the item drop animation so pieces visibly enter the containers and finish at their correct final positions.
- Increased task-completion modal storage space for the larger Piggy Bank and Treasure Chest.
