# Kudos Family 3.9.71

## Early lifetime upgrade

- During an active trial, the family or classroom creator now sees a gold **Upgrade to lifetime access now!** banner directly below the Settings header.
- The banner opens the existing verified StoreKit lifetime-purchase page without waiting for the trial to expire.
- The purchase page identifies the active trial, shows the remaining days, and provides a **Back to Settings** action.
- Invited adults without purchasing authority and child accounts do not see the upgrade banner.

## Sandbox purchase confirmation

Apple controls the purchase-success confirmation shown during Sandbox testing. Its Sandbox environment label is not generated by Kudos and does not appear in a live App Store purchase.

## Deployment

No database migration or Edge Function update is required for this release. A new app build is required because these are client interface and navigation changes.
