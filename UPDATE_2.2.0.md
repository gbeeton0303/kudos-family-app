# BrightSteps 2.2.0

- Appearance settings now support System, Light, and Dark modes and persist locally.
- Family Account opens the family/cloud management sheet.
- The dashboard family badge uses the first letter of the final word in the family workspace name.
- Profile, task, and reward photo controls offer Camera or Photo Library.
- Profile photos use stable source caching, prefetching, memoization, and zero fade delay for faster screen transitions.
