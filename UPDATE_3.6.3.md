# BrightSteps 3.6.3

Storage fill refinements:
- Piggy Bank fill is clipped to the round belly only.
- Piggy Bank currency is smaller and uses a stable irregular/overlapping pile instead of straight rows.
- Treasure Chest currency uses a stable irregular/overlapping pile.
- Treasure Chest fill floor was raised so currency cannot protrude below the bottom rail.
- Piggy Bank and Treasure Chest frames remain mounted during completion/replay animations to eliminate the brief borderless flash.
- Currency animation travel was shortened and driver usage normalized for smoother task-completion animation.
- Glass Jar behavior remains unchanged.
